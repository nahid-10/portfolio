How I decide what to automate Test strategy note, not a metric — the reasoning I actually apply before writing a test plan.
  1. Map flows by business risk, not by ease of testing. Checkout/payment and auth get priority over low-traffic admin screens, regardless of which is simpler to script.
  2. Automate what repeats; keep exploration where judgment matters. Stable, high-frequency regression paths go into Playwright/Postman suites. New features, edge cases, and anything UX-sensitive stay manual and exploratory until they stabilize.
  3. Triage by blast radius, not just severity label. A cosmetic bug on the payment page can outrank a "major" bug on a rarely-used settings screen — I ask who hits this, how often, and what it costs when they do.
  4. Wire the safety net into CI, not into memory. Regression suites run on every push/PR (see the Playwright project) so a fix can't silently break something else two weeks later.
